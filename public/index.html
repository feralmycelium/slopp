Each place has its own colours. Space to breathe. Motion you can stop. Your published drawings are shared under CC BY 4.0, credited to the slopp contributors (formerly Untranslatable). The code is MIT licensed.

Drag to turn. W/S move forward/back, A/D sideways, Q/E down/up. Arrow keys look around. M draws. Space pauses. Escape keeps your draft. Where sound is available, tap Turn sound on for an immersive soundscape. Drawing, moving and discovering traces trigger the sounds you hear. Tap Turn sound off to mute it.
