## Implementation decisions

- Keep saved drawing points, colours, rotations, IDs and database records.
  A deterministic display layout spaces existing contributions without a data
  migration. Earlier placements remain stable when later traces arrive.
- Use the same displayed position for rendering, navigation, sound proximity,
  stills and animation. Amended 16 October: new releases keep their drawing
  anchor for the current session without moving the camera, allowing Surprise
  to layer. Other visits use the shared spaced layout.
- Keep cream ground, serif invitation, abstract art and the six environments.
  Atlas references inspected: tezos-dragonfly-31759 (10, love, "beautiful")
  and no-art (10, love). Interpretation: give a composition room and keep the
  artwork dominant. No new taste law is inferred.
- Instructions remain until deliberately closed; Help reopens them. Store only
  the dismissal preference; Escape closes and restores focus. Keep the guide
  compact and non-modal. No extra camera animation for random jumps.
- Sound remains opt-in; its visible label and tap target persist during idle
  and drawing. Audio checks use headless Chromium with --mute-audio.
